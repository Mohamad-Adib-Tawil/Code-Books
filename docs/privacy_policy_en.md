# Privacy Policy for Code Book

Last updated: May 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the **Code Book** mobile application provided by **Mohamad Adib Tawil**. This policy explains how the app handles data when you use it, in alignment with Google Play and Apple App Store privacy and data transparency requirements.

## 1. Privacy Summary

Code Book is an app for browsing, discovering, and viewing book information using Google Books services. The app does not require users to create an account, and it does not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or personal files.

The app uses an internet connection to retrieve book data, such as title, author, description, cover image, category, and available preview or reading links from Google Books.

## 2. Data We Do Not Collect

Code Book does not collect, request, or store the following personal data:

- Name or personal identity information.
- Email address or phone number.
- Precise or approximate location.
- Contacts, call logs, or messages.
- Photos, camera, or microphone data.
- Payment or financial information.
- Health or sensitive data.
- User accounts or passwords.
- Advertising identifiers for tracking or personalized advertising.

## 3. Data Used by the App

The app may use the following data only to provide its core functionality:

- Search terms or categories entered by the user to search for books.
- Book data retrieved from Google Books, such as title, author, description, cover image, preview links, and availability information.
- Locally stored data on the device to improve the user experience, such as cached book lists or books viewed in the app.

We do not use this data to create a user profile, sell it, or share it with advertising networks.

## 4. Local Storage on the Device

The app uses local storage on the user’s device to save some book data, improve performance, and reduce the need to download the same data repeatedly.

This local storage may include:

- Book data downloaded from Google Books.
- Popular or newest book lists.
- Temporary data related to displaying books inside the app.

This data remains on the user’s device and is not uploaded to any server owned or operated by us. Users can delete this data at any time by clearing the app data from the device settings or uninstalling the app.

## 5. Internet Access and Third-Party Services

The app requires internet access to connect to Google Books API and to display book content or preview pages inside a WebView.

When using search features or opening a book reading or preview page, network requests may be sent to Google services. Google may process certain technical data required to provide the service, such as IP address, device or browser information inside WebView, and request logs, according to Google’s own policies.

You can review Google’s Privacy Policy here:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google Books pages may also be displayed inside WebView. Any data handled within those pages is subject to Google’s policies and terms.

## 6. Permissions

On Android, the app requests the following permission:

- `INTERNET`: used to connect to the internet, retrieve book data, and display Google Books pages.

The app does not request sensitive permissions such as location, camera, microphone, contacts, external storage, or notifications.

On iOS, the app does not request sensitive user permissions in its current version.

## 7. Cookies and WebView Technologies

Google Books pages displayed inside WebView may use cookies or similar technologies managed by Google. Code Book does not directly control these cookies and does not use them for its own advertising or tracking purposes.

If the user interacts with Google pages inside WebView, that interaction is governed by Google’s policies and terms of service.

## 8. Data Sharing

We do not sell user data and do not share personal data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Limited technical data may be shared with Google only as necessary to use Google Books API or display Google Books pages, as described in the Third-Party Services section.

We may disclose information if required by law or in response to a valid legal request, only to the extent required by applicable law.

## 9. Security

We use HTTPS connections when accessing Google Books services where available. Locally stored app data is kept within the app’s storage area on the user’s device according to the operating system’s mechanisms.

Although we use reasonable measures to protect data, no electronic transmission or storage method is 100% secure.

## 10. Data Retention and Deletion

Code Book does not store personal data on servers owned or operated by us and does not provide a user account system.

Locally stored app data remains on the user’s device until the user deletes it. Users can delete this data by:

- Clearing the app data from the device settings.
- Uninstalling the app from the device.

Because the app does not create accounts and does not store personal data on our servers, no account deletion process is required.

## 11. Children’s Privacy

Code Book is not primarily directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. If a parent or guardian believes that a child has provided personal data through the app, they may contact us so we can review the request and take appropriate action.

## 12. International Data Processing

Requests related to Google Books services may be processed on Google servers located in different countries. Such processing is governed by Google’s privacy policies and data protection practices.

## 13. User Rights

Depending on the laws applicable in your country, you may have rights related to accessing, deleting, or objecting to the processing of your data.

Because Code Book does not create user accounts and does not store personal data on servers owned or operated by us, most app-related data is stored locally on your device and can be deleted from the device settings or by uninstalling the app.

For any privacy-related requests, you can contact us using the contact details below.

## 14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time when app functionality changes, when new services are added, or to meet legal or store requirements.

The updated version will be published with a clear “Last updated” date. Continued use of the app after an update means that you accept the updated policy.
